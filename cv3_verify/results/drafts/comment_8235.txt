Measured this on three commits: c85f1a4 (before #8224), 1ea0ee3 (#8224) and bbee488.

Both scorers agree, wrong voice / requests, async_chunk mode:

  commit    K  C   wrong     rate    95% CI
  c85f1a4   2  1     0/120   0.0%   [0.000, 0.031]

So the leak reproduces before #8224 (Fisher p 3e-6 and 2e-13 against C=1) and is gone
from 1ea0ee3 on. Text stays correct in the leaked outputs (mean WER < 0.01), only the
speaker changes. Every leaked voice belonged to a request that was in flight at the
same time. With 8 voices, the leaked voice was that of the request sent just before or
after in 70 of 90 cases (about 26 expected by chance) and that of the earliest
in-flight request in 13 (about 11 expected). That is from client timestamps only, but
it points at conditioning landing one batch row off rather than always falling back
to row 0.

--no-async-chunk could not be measured: it fails on the first request at all three
commits (see the note on #8262).

Setup: 1x RTX 4090 24 GB, driver 580.95.05, Ubuntu 22.04, Python 3.12, vllm 0.30.0, torch 2.13.0+cu130,
Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512, stock deploy/cosyvoice3.yaml (talker max_num_seqs 8, FLASH_ATTN),
vllm-omni installed editable from a worktree of each commit.

Method: voices registered with POST /v1/audio/voices, 120 seeded /v1/audio/speech requests
per run, round-robin over K voices, four English texts of different lengths. Every output
is scored against all K references with two independent speaker models (CAM++ campplus.onnx
from the model, and WavLM-base-plus-SV); a leak counts only when both name the same wrong
voice. Whisper-small WER and duration ratio for corrupted output.

Kit: https://github.com/Arifuzzamanjoy/vllm-omni/tree/repro/cosyvoice3-voice-leak-8235

Caveats: the FlashInfer sampler could not JIT on this machine (no nvcc), so all servers ran
with VLLM_USE_FLASHINFER_SAMPLER=0 (PyTorch top-k/top-p), same for every commit; neither bug
path goes through the sampler. One seed at C=1 per cell. Single GPU class.
